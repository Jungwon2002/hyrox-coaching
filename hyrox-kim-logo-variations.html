Logo · 6 Variations · HYROX KIM

Six Directions, One Mark

Each direction keeps the brand's core — Oswald hairline Latin, Pretendard Korean, ink + volt palette — while exploring a different spatial relationship between the name and the slash. The current "Split" logo is included as Direction 0 for comparison.

Current · Direction 0 — The Split
0 The Split
What it is: HYROX and KIM sit on one line separated by a skewed yellow parallelogram — the site's signature motif. Strong, instantly recognizable, and the identity the site is built on. The slash is the recurring brand mark.
Direction 1
1 The Bracket vertical · stacked · architectural
Idea: A volt vertical rule anchors the left edge like a starting block. HYROX runs full-width at the top, KIM sits beneath it — the name reads as two stacked claims, not one fused word. The bracket gives it a structural, almost architectural feel. Works especially well as a nav lockup (compact version drops the subtitle).
Direction 2
2 The Strike-through one line · line-through · bold
Idea: HYROX KIM is written as one continuous word. A volt line strikes through the top of the name — a finish line, a tape, a barrier broken. The mark is the interruption itself. Reads fastest of all six directions; the most compact in nav bars and avatars.
Direction 3
3 The Stack two-weight · layered · editorial
Idea: HYROX runs light and wide (Oswald 200, wide tracking); KIM runs heavy and tight (Anton, the wordmark's original face). The volt line beneath KIM is the signature slash turned horizontal — a ground line, a starting position. Most editorial of the six, with clear hierarchy. The tension between the two weights makes the name feel like a statement.
Direction 4
4 The V-Cut diagonal cut · dynamic · slice
Idea: A skewed volt wedge cuts into the left edge — the slash motif, but larger, more aggressive, a starting chute or a lane divider. HYROX and KIM stack on two lines to the right of the cut. The whole mark reads as something in motion; the wedge implies forward velocity. Most dynamic of the six.
Direction 5
5 The Shield contained · crest · credible
Idea: A compact crest-like composition: HYROX above KIM, with a small skewed volt wedge as the left shoulder mark — a badge, not a divider. Feels the most "official," like an event seal or a federation mark. Reads as credible and established. The wedge is the same geometry as the site's eyebrow tick, scaled down to badge size.
Direction 6
6 The Signal Bar split · signal · precise
Idea: Closest to the current Split, but refined: HYROX and KIM sit on one line with a tall volt rectangle between them — a signal bar, a timing gate, a lane marker. A hairline rule beneath grounds the whole mark. The bar is taller and thinner than the current parallelogram, making the separation read as a deliberate pause rather than an inserted shape. Most precise of the six.

How to read these: Each direction is a complete mark, not a mood board. Test them at small sizes (nav bar, avatar), on dark backgrounds (hero cover), and next to the site's existing typography. The current Split logo (Direction 0) is the bar — any replacement should earn its place by being at least as clear at 24px height and at least as recognizable in a single color. Directions 2 and 3 are the strongest contenders for readability at small sizes; Direction 6 is the closest evolution of the current mark with minimal disruption to the existing site.
